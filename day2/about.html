About QuickNotes

QuickNotes is a lightweight tool designed to help you jot down ideas quickly and stay organized. It’s perfect for students, professionals, or anyone who needs a fast way to capture thoughts.

How to use QuickNotes

  1. Open QuickNotes and create a new note.
  2. Type your ideas or tasks into the editor.
  3. Save and review your notes whenever you need them.

Features

  • Simple and clean interface
  • Automatic saving of notes
  • Keyboard shortcuts for speed

Keyboard shortcuts

Shortcut Action
Ctrl+N Create a new note
Ctrl+S Save current note
